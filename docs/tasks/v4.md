# Gymatch V4 — Pending Roadmap

Everything that remains to be built. Consolidates the open items from V2.2, V2.4, and V3.
Features already shipped (V2, V2.1, V2.4 A/B/D/E) are not listed here.

## C — Gym Pages *(was V2.4 C)*

Every gym (from Google Places data) gets a public page.

- Name + address + map (from existing Google Places data)
- Photos gallery (Google Places photos at MVP)
- Hours — opening hours from Google Places + member-sourced training schedule (from shipped D)
- Sports / disciplines — derived from member `disciplines` data + manual curation
- **Gymbros** — "Users who train here": live count + avatar list
  - Counts users active in the last 30 days with this gym set as their gym
  - Privacy: users with hidden/incognito profiles never appear
  - Clicking a gymbro opens their profile → swipe/match entry point
- **Upcoming events** (already shipped in V2.4 E), with quick filters: Open mats / Competitions / All

**Reviews and publisher rights ship with D (Gym Verification)**, not in the first slice.

## F — Discovery Enhancements *(was V3)*

**Boost** — pay to appear at the top of the discovery feed for a set window (30 min / 3h / 24h). Visible badge while active.

**Top Picks** — daily curated set of 10 algorithmically selected profiles (shared gym, overlapping schedule, same discipline). Refreshes every 24h.

**Passport** — temporarily change location to browse and match in another city or country.

**Location-Based Discovery (PostGIS)** — filter by real distance. Approximate GPS coordinates (rounded ~500m for privacy), max radius filter, `ST_DWithin`-based feed queries. Requires PostGIS extension in Supabase.

**Smart Photos** — automatically reorder profile photos based on right-swipe rate. A/B tested per viewer session.

---

## G — Engagement Mechanics *(was V3)*

**Daily Swipe Limit** — free users get a limited number of likes per day (e.g. 20). Feature flag infrastructure already exists.

**Rewind** — undo the last swipe. Feature flag already exists. Limited uses for free / unlimited for paid.

**Super Like** — strong interest signal visible to the other user before they decide. Feature flag already exists. Limited for free / unlimited for paid.

**Likes Received** — blurred list of who liked you. Unblur requires premium.

**Activity Status** — green dot or "Active recently" label on cards.

---

## H — Profile & Social Proof *(was V3)*

**Verified Badge** — selfie matching profile photo (reuses B). Blue checkmark visible on card.

**Fitness Tags / Interests** — hashtag-style tags: `#madrugador #crossfit #PR_hunter`. Filterable in discovery.

**Prompts** — short questions with custom answers shown on the card:
- "My current PR is…"
- "I'm looking for someone to…"
- "My rest day looks like…"

**Spotify Integration** — connect Spotify to show workout anthem or current favorite track on profile.

**Instagram Grid** — optional: show last 6 posts (public account only) for social proof.

---

## I — Monetization *(was V3)*

**Gymatch Plus** — monthly subscription:
- Unlimited swipes
- Rewind (unlimited)
- 1 Super Like / day
- Passport
- Ad-free

**Gymatch Gold** — premium tier above Plus:
- See who liked you (unblurred)
- Top Picks access
- Priority in discovery algorithm

**À la Carte** — one-off purchases:
- Extra Boosts (pack of 3 / 5 / 10)
- Extra Super Likes (pack of 5)
- One-time Passport pass

---

## J — Chat Enhancements *(was V3)*

- **GIFs** — Giphy integration in the chat composer
- **Icebreakers** — AI-suggested conversation starters based on shared interests, shown before the first message

---

## K — Community *(was V3)*

**Training Groups** — create or join groups around a gym, discipline, or schedule slot. Group chat included. Example: "CrossFit Box Palermo — WOD crew".

**Social Feed** — opt-in chronological feed of workout updates from your matches (session type, muscle group, gym). Not algorithmic.

---

## Out of Scope (deferred past V4)

- AI personal training recommendations
- Wearable integrations (Garmin, Apple Watch, Strava)
- Marketplace (supplements, gear)
- Verified coach profiles
- In-app workout logging / tracking
- Data exports (RDF/XML, JSON-LD, CSV, RSS)
- Gym business dashboard (billing, multi-branch, analytics)
- Dark mode

---

7. **G** — Engagement Mechanics (activates monetization pressure)
8. **I** — Monetization
9. **H** — Profile & Social Proof
10. **J** — Chat Enhancements
